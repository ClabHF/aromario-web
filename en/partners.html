Partners

For stores, affiliates and creators

This page is for perfume stores, affiliate networks and programs, and content creators who want to work with Aromario.

1.What Aromario is

Aromario is a digital perfume wardrobe: users save their collection and the app recommends every morning which perfume to wear, based on the weather, the occasion and their profile. It is free, with no subscriptions, and available on Google Play; the iPhone version launches on the App Store on November 14, 2026.

2.The catalog

The catalog holds more than 20,000 perfumes with a technical profile (notes, accords, longevity and sillage): designer, niche, Arabic and more. Each profile can link to the stores that sell it.

3.The audience

The app is used mainly in the United States (including the Spanish-speaking community) and in Spain, with an audience in Latin America too. The website is published in 14 languages.

4.How affiliation works

When a user taps Buy on a profile, the app opens the store with an affiliate link. If they buy, the store pays Aromario a commission, at no extra cost to the user. We work with networks such as Awin, CJ and Rakuten Advertising, and we only link to stores that sell and ship in the user’s country.

5.Creators

If you create perfume content and want to try Aromario or prepare an honest review, write to us. We do not pay for positive opinions: we are looking for honest reviews.
