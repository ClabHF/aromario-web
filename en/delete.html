Your account

Delete your account and data

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1.From the app (fastest)

Open Aromario and go to Profile › Delete my account and data. Confirm, and your account, profile and lists are deleted right away, in the cloud and on your phone. You need an internet connection; if you signed in a long time ago, the app will ask you to choose your Google or Apple account again.

2.By email (if you no longer have the app)

Write to clabhfdevelopment@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Aromario account”, from the email associated with your Google or Apple account. If you signed in with Apple using a hidden email, write to us from that relay address. We will do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Email us to delete my account

3.What is deleted

  • Your profile (your quiz answers).
  • Your wardrobe and wishlist stored in the cloud.
  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).
  • From the app, also everything stored on your phone.

4.What is kept

  • The anonymous statistics counters, which can’t be linked to you.
  • If you ask by email, a minimal record of the request (your email and the date) for 12 months, for legal and security reasons. It isn’t used for anything else.
