Your account

Delete your account and data

Last updated: October 16, 2026

1.From the app (fastest)

Open Aromario and go to Profile › Delete my account and data. Confirm, and your account, profile and lists are deleted right away, in the cloud and on your phone. You need an internet connection; if you signed in a long time ago, the app will ask you to choose your Google account again.

2.By email (if you no longer have the app)

Write to clabhfdevelopment@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Aromario account”, from the same Google email you signed in with. We will do it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Email us to delete my account

3.What is deleted

  • Your profile (your quiz answers).
  • Your wardrobe and wishlist stored in the cloud.
  • Your sign-in account (Firebase Authentication).
  • From the app, also everything stored on your phone.

4.What is kept

  • The anonymous statistics counters, which can’t be linked to you.
  • If you ask by email, a minimal record of the request (your email and the date) for 12 months, for legal and security reasons. It isn’t used for anything else.
