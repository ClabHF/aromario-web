Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

1.Information we collect

To use AROMARIO you must sign in with your Google account; it is the only way to access the app and we do not offer a guest mode. We collect the following information:

  • Your Google account data: your name, email address and profile picture, which we use to identify you and give you access to the app.
  • Your fragrance profile: the initial survey answers (your occasions, preferences and the location/weather settings you configure) are synced with Firebase to back up your account.
  • Your wardrobe and your wishlist: are stored only on your phone. They never leave your device, are never synced to the cloud, and are never deleted when you update the app.

2.Use of information

The information you log is used solely to provide the app's functionality: organizing your collection, keeping your scent journal, and offering the daily fragrance recommendation based on the weather and your plans. We never sell or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

3.Anonymous statistics

We may collect aggregated, anonymous statistics about app usage (for example, which features are used most) to improve the service. These data never identify you personally and are never sold to third parties.

4.Third-party services

The app uses third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication: for signing in with your Google account.
  • Cloud Firestore: to back up your fragrance profile in the cloud.
  • Google Play Services: for Android system functions.

We recommend reviewing Google's privacy policies on these providers' official sites.

5.Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, both on your device and in the cloud. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6.Children's privacy

AROMARIO is intended for people 18 years of age or older and does not knowingly collect information from minors. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a minor has provided us with information, contact us to delete it.

7.Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by publishing the new version on this page with its update date.

8.Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, write to us at clabhfdevelopment@gmail.com.
