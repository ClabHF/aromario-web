FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is Aromario free?

Yes. When you buy a perfume through a link in the app, the store may pay us a commission, at no extra cost to you.

Do I need an account?

You sign in with your Google account. No new passwords needed.

Where are my perfumes stored?

On your phone. Since version 1.0.108, if you’re signed in, your wardrobe and wishlist are also backed up to the cloud with your account, so you don’t lose them when you change phones.

How does it pick my perfume of the day?

It checks your city’s weather hour by hour, the season, the occasion you choose and your profile. It also rotates your collection so it isn’t always the same one.

What is “To discover”?

A daily suggestion of a perfume you don’t own that suits your taste, so you can grow your collection.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. In the app, under Profile › Delete my account and data, or from the account deletion page.

Which phones does it work on?

Android for now. The iPhone version will come later.
