Features

Your entire fragrance universe, in one place

AROMARIO combines the elegance of a classic wardrobe with the intelligence of modern technology.

Digital wardrobe

Log your collection with the full profile of each perfume: fragrance notes, accords, longevity, sillage, and your impressions. Your wardrobe, always with you.

Daily recommendation

Every morning, the ideal fragrance based on the weather, your plans, and the season. The scent of the day stays fixed so the app, widget, and notification always match.

Layer perfumes

Discover which fragrances pair well together: the app analyzes shared notes and accords and tells you in which order to apply them for a perfect result.

Wishlist

Save the fragrances you want to try, with a direct buy button to the store. Your next favorites, organized and one tap away.
